## Course Completion: Bringing the Six Pillars Together

The six pillars work as one system. TARGET without EXPRESSION becomes a management plan that people may not challenge. EXPRESSION without ACCOUNTABILITY produces good conversations without reliable follow-through. ACCOUNTABILITY without MINDSET can become blame. MINDSET without INSIGHT can become unstructured experimentation. INSIGHT without QUALITY can optimise short-term value at the expense of customers and long-term cost. QUALITY without TARGET can perfect work that never mattered.

A team becomes predictably high performing when it can run the complete TEAM IQ Measurement Cycle: set a measurable target; communicate evidence and learning; own outcomes; test and learn; then decide and improve from value, risk, and quality data. The work is not about applying every tool at once. Start where the team has the greatest constraint, build evidence of improvement, and use the learning to decide the next move.

Final TEAM IQ commitment: We will not measure effort as a substitute for value. We will not hide uncertainty as a substitute for confidence. We will not wait for permission to think. We will use our collective intelligence to create the right outcome together.
