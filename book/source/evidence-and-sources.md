Lets start with MIT. Who likes doing studies?

MIT like doing studies its what makes them famous for start. They did a couple of studies that checked the correlation between the best and most high performing teams and individual IQs in the team.

After 2 years if study of 700 people they published in 2010 Science Journal that there was NO significant statistical CORRELATION between IQ and Team performance.

Google thought exactly what you are thinking, that’s got to be wrong? because isn’t that exactly how we recruit? Must have a degree in XYZ. If I add some Genius level person to my team surely it must increase the performance??? So in 2011 they started their own study, studying 1000’s people and teams across google looking for what made up highly effective teams, looking for the correlation to the IQ of the individuals in the team and after 4 years they found - no significant statistical correlation to TEAM performance.
