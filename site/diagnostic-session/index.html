02 WHY THIS STEP

Most organisations do not need a programme. They need an accurate diagnosis.

Buying team development on faith is how organisations end up with a shelf of workshops and no evidence of change. So we made the first paid step small, specific and useful on its own.

Ninety minutes is long enough to gather real evidence — who owns what, what the target actually is, where the decisions are stuck — and short enough that approving it does not require a procurement cycle.

03 HOW IT RUNS

Three stages, and what you get from each

Before · 20 minutes of your time

Scope the area

You tell us the concern in one paragraph. We agree who needs to be in the room — usually the team, plus whoever owns the constraint. We send a short pre-read so nobody arrives cold.

During · 90 minutes

Work the problem

We run the measurement cycle on the live issue: what the valuable target is, what evidence exists, who owns the outcome, what is being assumed, and where the decision has actually stopped. No theory without the team's own example.

After · within five working days

The written output

A Team IQ Score for the team, the pillar where the constraint sits, a prioritised action plan, and the two exercises from that pillar's chapters to start on. A sample of the report format is available.

05 QUESTIONS

The practical questions

Who needs to attend?

The team that owns the problem, plus whoever holds the constraint — often someone from outside the team. Between four and ten people works best. If it is just you and the problem is systemic, that is still useful but we will say so.

Is it a workshop or a diagnosis?

Both. The first half gathers evidence and scores the team against the relevant pillars. The second half works on the constraint with the people who own it.

What if we need more than one area?

Then the corporate diagnostic across all six pillars is usually the better next step, and the £1,200 is credited against it.

Can this be run remotely?

Yes, and most are. In-person is better for the exercise, so we will say if we think it would change the outcome.

Will I get anything if it does not work for us?

You keep the written score, the action plan and the two exercises. Nothing is withheld pending a proposal.
