05 QUESTIONS

Before you register

Is it a sales presentation?

No. One pillar, one tool, one exercise, questions. There is a five-minute close at the end for anyone who wants to go further, and you are welcome to leave before it.

Who attends?

Mostly heads of delivery, transformation leads, PMO leads and engineering managers from organisations running several teams. Occasionally a COO.

Can I bring my team?

Yes, and it is better if you do. Registration is per person so we can send everyone the joining link and the notes.

What if I cannot make the live session?

Register anyway. Everyone who registers gets the replay for 14 days and the attendee notes permanently.

How often does this run?

First Tuesday of every month. Monthly to begin with, and we will add dates if demand justifies it.
