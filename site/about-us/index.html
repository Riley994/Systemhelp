01 ABOUT

We built this because the standard answer never worked.

Twenty-eight years of delivering programmes produced the same conclusion repeatedly: the framework was rarely the problem, and the people were rarely the problem. The team's ability to think together was the problem — and nobody was measuring it.

02 WHERE IT CAME FROM

From delivering programmes to measuring the thing that decided them.

Systemhelp has delivered technology and change programmes for organisations across several sectors — including work recognised with a Computing Cloud Project of the Year award and an Nvidia Best Newcomer award.

After enough programmes, a pattern becomes impossible to ignore. The teams with the best individuals did not reliably win. The teams with the newest framework did not reliably win. The teams that could adapt together — that surfaced bad news early, made decisions near the information, and got better after every failure — did.

Then the research caught up with the observation. MIT found that individual intelligence does not predict team performance, and identified a collective-intelligence factor instead. Google spent four years looking for the same thing and found that it comes down to how the team works, not who is in it.

So we built an instrument to measure it, and a method to raise it. TEAM IQ Creator is the result: six pillars, nineteen chapters, fourteen tools, one measurement cycle — and a re-measurement that tells you whether any of it worked.

In one line

We measure how well a team thinks together, raise the weakest pillar first, and prove the change on the same scale.

05 QUESTIONS

About us

Are you a training company?

No. Training is one component of a programme, but the product is measurement and capability. If all you want is a course, the free monthly workshop and the book cover most of it.

Do you use contractors?

Occasionally for specialist facilitation, always named in advance and always working alongside one of us rather than instead of us.

What sectors do you work in?

The method is sector-independent, because the constraint is collective intelligence rather than domain knowledge. Most enquiries come from technology, financial services, engineering and professional services organisations of 200 to 2,000 people.

Why is the site in pounds sterling?

We are UK-based, VAT-registered, and most of our customers are UK businesses that reclaim the VAT. Quoting in pounds with VAT shown separately is simply the honest way to price.
