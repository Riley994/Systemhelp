What the alternative costs

The cost of doing nothing is not zero — it is simply unbudgeted. It appears as three lines that rarely reach a board paper:

Rework Effort spent producing something that then has to be redone. Typically around 15% of delivery capacity, and never reported, because it already sits inside the plan.
Decision delay Capacity blocked while a decision waits for a forum far from the information. Days per decision, multiplied across every team.
Stalled work Initiatives that consumed budget and then quietly stopped. Each one is a funded business case that produced nothing.

The full model, with every assumption editable so finance can interrogate it rather than dismiss it, is at systemhelp.co.uk/board-case/#calculator. At typical values for ten teams the annual figure lands close to £1m.

Why this is not the usual team-development spend
