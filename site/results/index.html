02 OUR POSITION

We are publishing the method before the case studies.

TEAM IQ Creator is a measured method, and measurement cuts both ways. To publish an outcome, we need a baseline, a re-measure, and written consent from the organisation. That takes time, and we would rather be late than invent one.

What that means for you in practice:

  • You get the method today. The six pillars, the fourteen tools and the measurement cycle are fully specified and available.
  • You get the evidence base. Every claim on this site is published research with a citation, listed on the evidence page.
  • You become the case study. Our first cohort of published outcomes will be organisations who measured properly from the start. If that is you, the terms will say so in advance and in writing.

We have removed testimonials from the old website that we could not verify, and we have removed every statistic we could not source. That includes a widely repeated figure about rework reduction that we previously displayed and no longer can.

What we will never do

  • Publish an outcome without a measured baseline and a re-measure
  • Attribute a team's improvement to one individual
  • Use a client's name or data without written consent
  • Quote a statistic we cannot link to

Where the method comes from

TEAM IQ Creator was built from the pattern that kept repeating: capable people, working hard, inside an operating model that could not use their combined intelligence. Twenty-eight years of delivery produced a lot of examples of that, and a growing frustration that the standard answer — a new framework — never fixed it.

The motorsport bit

Nine championships across a career in motorsport teaches the same lesson as a delivery programme: preparation is only half of it, and the team that adapts fastest when the plan breaks wins. Both disciplines reward collective intelligence and punish the lack of it.
