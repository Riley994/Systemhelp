03 THE SMALL PRINT

Terms, plainly

Currency and VAT

All prices are in pounds sterling. VAT is charged at the prevailing UK rate and shown separately on every invoice. Businesses registered for VAT can normally reclaim it.

Payment

Card or bank transfer for the diagnostic session. Larger engagements are invoiced in three stages: on signature, at the midpoint, and on delivery of the closing report.

Credits and refunds

The £1,200 diagnostic fee is credited in full against a corporate diagnostic or programme within six months. If a diagnostic session has to be cancelled with less than 48 hours' notice, we rebook once at no cost.

What is not included

Travel and accommodation for on-site work are recharged at cost. Third-party tools or licences are never bundled in without being named first.

Scope changes

If the number of teams changes materially, we re-scope in writing before doing the extra work. No surprise invoices.

Your data

Scores and evidence belong to you. Benchmarks are anonymised and aggregated before use. See the privacy policy.

05 QUESTIONS

Pricing questions

Why is the diagnostic session so much cheaper than the rest?

Because it is deliberately a low-risk first paid step. It is priced to be approved by a single manager without procurement, and it is credited back if you continue.

Do you offer discounts for charities or the public sector?

Yes, on a case-by-case basis. Tell us the context when you enquire and we will be straight about what is possible.

Can we pay monthly?

For the retained partnership, yes. For the programme, we normally invoice in three stages, which can be aligned to your financial year if that helps.

What if we only want the diagnostic and nothing else?

That is a complete product, not a teaser. The plan is written to be executed without us and the fee is simply what it costs.

Are prices going up?

They will, as the benchmark cohort grows and the materials mature. Anything quoted in writing is held for 90 days.
