04 QUESTIONS

About the book

Who is it written for?

Delivery directors, heads of transformation, PMO leads and COOs in organisations running more than one team. It assumes you have already met Agile and are past being impressed by it.

Is it a sales book?

There is one chapter at the end describing how we work with organisations. The rest stands on its own — the exercises and tools are free to use internally whether or not you ever speak to us.

Does it repeat the website?

It goes further. The site gives you the argument and the scorecard; the book gives you the chapters, the tools and the exercises in full.
