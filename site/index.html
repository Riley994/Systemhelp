04 THE METHOD

Six pillars. One measurement cycle. A number that moves.

TEAM IQ Creator scores a team across six pillars. Each pillar has four chapters, a set of named measurement tools, and exercises that produce working artefacts — not workshop notes.

TARGET

A valuable result the whole team can see, with a baseline, a target and a review date.

EXPRESSION

Communication that creates understanding rather than volume — safety, clarity, listening, conflict.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Shared certainty about who owns which outcome, what evidence proves it, and how handoffs work.

MINDSET

How the team reads challenge, uncertainty, failure and difference — and whether it tests or debates.

INSIGHT

Sound, timely decisions made where the information is richest, with triggers that force a re-decide.

QUALITY

The right value, to the right standard, at the right cost and time — measured including rework.

The measurement cycle runs through every pillar: Set a measurable target · Communicate what you know and what you are assuming · Own the outcome and its evidence · Learn by testing the riskiest assumption · Decide and improve from value, risk and quality data. Fourteen named tools sit inside those five stages.

09 QUESTIONS

The questions we are asked before anyone signs anything.

How is this different from team-building or a personality assessment?

Team-building changes how people feel for a day. Personality tools describe individuals. TEAM IQ measures how the team thinks together, scores it, and re-measures it after you have changed something. If the number does not move, we have not finished.

Do we have to run a full programme to get value?

No. Most organisations start with the £1,200 diagnostic session on one area of concern. You leave with a written Team IQ Score and a prioritised action plan, and that fee is credited in full against a programme if you continue.

How long does a corporate programme take?

Twelve to sixteen weeks across all six pillars, with training and exercises for each team, dashboards so you can watch the scores move, and quarterly re-measurement.

Will this survive a board scrutiny?

That is the design goal. Every claim on this site is cited, the cost-of-inaction model shows all of its assumptions, and the sample diagnostic report shows the board exactly what they will receive before you commit.

We have already done Agile, SAFe and a transformation. Why now?

Because the process was never the constraint. If your framework was the answer, delivery would be predictable by now. TEAM IQ works on the team that has to operate the framework when reality does not match the plan.
