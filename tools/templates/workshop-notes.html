From conversation to evidence

Worked example: EXPRESSION measurement cycle

Cycle stepExample in live work
1. NoticeA delivery team repeatedly says, “the customer will prefer the new approval journey,” with no shared evidence source.
2. BaselineSample five material claims in planning and review meetings. Only one contains evidence, a stated assumption and a defined test.
3. PractiseAdopt the CES: label each claim as evidence, assumption, hypothesis or test plan; record it where the team can find it.
4. Re-measureAfter two weeks, sample five claims again. Review the percentage meeting all CES conditions and one decision changed by the new evidence.
5. AdaptKeep the practice that improved clarity; simplify the route if it created delay or excluded useful voices.

Four questions to take back to the team

  1. What outcome are we trying to change, and what is our current baseline?
  2. Which part of our current story is evidence, and which part is an assumption?
  3. What is the smallest safe test that could reduce the most important uncertainty?
  4. Where will we record the decision, owner, evidence and date we will review it?
