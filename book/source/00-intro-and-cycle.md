## TEAM IQ Creator®

### Full Core Course Content: The Six Pillars of Predictable High Performance

Audience: Leaders, managers, coaches, delivery professionals, and intact teams.
Format: Modular LMS learning, facilitated workshops, or a blended programme.
Course scope: The six TEAM IQ Creator® pillars and their 19 sub-area chapters.

### How to Use This Course

TEAM IQ Creator® is a practical system for building teams that can think, decide, learn, and deliver together under conditions of speed, complexity, and uncertainty. The course is not a process to be imposed on a team. It is a set of repeatable practices that improve the team's collective capability to create the right response when the process, plan, or individual expert is not enough.

Each chapter follows the same learning rhythm. Participants first understand the principle, then see how it appears in real work, connect it to research, measure it using a TEAM IQ tool, and finish by applying it to a live team challenge. The exercises are designed to create usable working artefacts rather than classroom-only discussion.

TEAM IQ principle: A high-performing team is not a group of high-performing individuals. It is a group whose members can reliably create better outcomes together than they could create alone.

### The TEAM IQ Measurement Cycle

The course uses fourteen TEAM IQ measurement tools. They are not a separate reporting layer. They make the team's intentions, evidence, ownership, learning, decisions, and outcomes visible.
