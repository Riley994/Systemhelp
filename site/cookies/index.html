What we use

This site sets no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It does not use a cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to. We do not use cookies to follow you around this or other websites.

Scorecard form state

The only browser storage used is what is needed to remember form state on the TEAM IQ Scorecard while it runs. This prevents your answers being lost as you move through the questions. It is not used for advertising, analytics or tracking.

Browser controls

You can inspect, delete or block cookies and browser storage in your browser settings. The usual places are:

  • Chrome: Settings, Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies.
  • Edge: Settings, Cookies and site permissions, then Manage and delete cookies and site data.
  • Firefox: Settings, Privacy & Security, then Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Settings, Privacy, then Manage Website Data.

Blocking all browser storage may stop the scorecard from remembering answers during a session.
