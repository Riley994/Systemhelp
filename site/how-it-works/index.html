01 THE METHOD

Six pillars. One measurement cycle. A score that moves.

TEAM IQ Creator is not a workshop and it is not a framework. It is a way of scoring how well a team thinks together, applying named tools to the weakest pillar first, and then proving the improvement by re-measuring.

03 THE PILLARS

What the six pillars actually cover

Each pillar is taught in chapters, each chapter ends in a measurement task, and each produces a working artefact rather than a discussion.

Pillar 1 · 4 chapters

TARGET — a clear target related to the team, with a reward

Definition: TARGET is the team's shared line of sight to a valuable result. It is not a wish, not a broad corporate ambition and not a task list. In its four chapters it covers TRUE, ACHIEVABLE, REQUIRED, GIVE, EXPLAINABLE and TRACEABLE.

  • T · True — the real required date, not a wish or a nice-to-have. Measurement: T.A.R.G.E.T Value Index (TVI).
  • A · Achievable — feasibility reviewed by the people who will do the work: complexity, uncertainty, capacity, and the effort quality needs. Measurement: Value-to-Effort Ratio (VER).
  • R · Required — whether the work should exist at all, traced to customer, business or risk value. Measurement: Value-to-Effort Ratio (VER).
  • G · Give — the reward component, and why effort without recognition decays. Measurement: G.I.V.E Recognition Signal.
  • E · Explainable — defining the destination so plainly that anyone can repeat it. Measurement: T.A.R.G.E.T Value Index (TVI).
  • T · Traceable — proving success and reducing uncertainty. Measurement: Target Risk Burn-Down (TRB).

Pillar 2 · 4 chapters

EXPRESSION — have great communication

Definition: EXPRESSION is the team's ability to exchange information, ideas, concerns, uncertainty, disagreement and learning in a way that is safe, clear, balanced and useful. Not the volume of communication — the quality of understanding it creates.

  • 2.1 Create safety and lead by example — seven behaviours in the C.L.E.A.R.L.Y Toolkit. Measurement: C.L.E.A.R.L.Y Evidence Standard (CES).
  • 2.2 Expectations — the clarity code and the 5 C's. Measurement: Expression of Value Delta (EVD).
  • 2.3 Active listening (E.A.R.S) and regular team building. Measurement: Expression of Value Delta (EVD).
  • 2.4 L.E.A.N conflict resolution and Yin Yang feedback — disagreement as a resource. Measurement: C.L.E.A.R.L.Y Evidence Standard (CES).

Pillar 3 · 2 chapters

ACCOUNTABILITY — shared certainty about who owns what

Definition: ACCOUNTABILITY is the team's shared certainty about who owns which outcomes, what evidence will demonstrate progress, and how handoffs work.

  • 3.1 Define roles and model accountability. Measurement: D.R.E.A.M Outcome Contract (DOC).
  • 3.2 Regular check-ins, empowerment and agreements. Measurement: Quantified D.R.E.A.M Boundaries (QDB).

Pillar 4 · 3 chapters

MINDSET — how the team reads challenge and failure

Definition: MINDSET is the team's shared way of interpreting challenge, uncertainty, failure, difference and opportunity — the belief that capability can be built.

  • 4.1 Make a growth mindset and innovate. Measurement: M.I.N.D.S.E.T Value Engineering (MVE).
  • 4.2 Networking and distributed shared ownership. Measurement: Risk-First Learning Loops (RLL).
  • 4.3 Synergy, education and team tributes. Measurement: M.I.N.D.S.E.T Value Engineering (MVE).

Pillar 5 · 3 chapters

INSIGHT — sound decisions where the information is richest

Definition: INSIGHT is the team's capability to make sound, timely, evidence-aware decisions at the point where information is richest.

  • 5.1 Decentralised decision making and purpose-based leadership. Measurement: D.I.R.E.C.T Value Routing (DVR).
  • 5.2 Interconnected metrics and regular feedback loops. Measurement: 7 C's Outcome Metrics (COM).
  • 5.3 Clear channels and transparent information sharing. Measurement: Insight Risk Triggers (IRT).

Pillar 6 · 3 chapters

QUALITY — the right value, standard, cost and time

Definition: QUALITY is the team's ability to deliver the right value, to the right standard, at the right cost and at the right time. It is not polish.

  • 6.1 Continue with the end in mind and value stream mapping. Measurement: 7 C's Value Engineering Standard (CVE).
  • 6.2 Customer-centric design and continuous feedback. Measurement: 7 C's Outcome Metrics (COM).
  • 6.3 Corrective QA, cost analysis and cross-functional collaboration. Measurement: D.I.R.E.C.T Decision Audits (DDA).

04 IN PRACTICE

What actually happens in a diagnostic

90 minutes on one area of concern, with the people who own it in the room.
1 written Team IQ Score and a prioritised action plan, in your hands the same week.
2 exercises your team can start on immediately, drawn from the weakest pillar's chapters.

Before

You tell us the area that hurts. We send a short pre-read and agree who needs to be in the room — usually the team plus whoever owns the constraint.

During

We work the problem in the room: what the target actually is, what evidence exists, who owns the outcome, what is being assumed, and where the decision is stuck.

After

A written score, the two starting exercises, and a plan you can run without us. If you continue into a programme, the £1,200 is credited in full.

05 QUESTIONS

Method questions

Do we have to use all six pillars?

No. Six pillars is the full model, but the starting point is always the one with the greatest constraint. Organisations routinely start with TARGET or EXPRESSION and come back to the rest.

How long before we see a change?

The first measurable movement normally appears within four to six weeks, because the early changes are about evidence and ownership rather than culture. The programme re-measures quarterly.

Is this compatible with our existing framework?

Yes, and it is designed that way. TEAM IQ raises the capability of the people operating Agile, SAFe, waterfall or anything else you have. We do not replace your process.

How do you measure something as soft as "mindset"?

Through behaviour and evidence, not attitude surveys. For example: how many ideas were tested this quarter, how long an assumption stayed unexamined, and whether people ask for help across team boundaries. Each pillar has named tools that make this concrete.

Can you work with just one team?

Yes — that is the pillar sprint. The larger diagnostics and programmes are for organisations with several teams that need a comparable measure across all of them.
